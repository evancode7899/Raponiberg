# Contributing

**This repository doesn't take contributions.** Pull requests and change suggestions
aren't accepted, and only the maintainer changes this copy of Raponiberg.

That's on purpose: Raponiberg is meant to be **downloaded and made your own**. Clone it (or
fork it on GitHub), then change anything you like in your copy: new pages, your own
categories, a parser for your bank. The [LICENSE](LICENSE) allows that freely, with two
conditions: the app stays named exactly Raponiberg, with nothing added, and nobody makes
money from it.

The README's [Making it yours](README.md#making-it-yours) section covers:
- how to customize your copy safely
- how to keep your financial data out of anything you publish
- how to bring in the maintainer's later releases if you want them
